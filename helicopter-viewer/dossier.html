File 01 / 04Overview

Shrouded rotor system

The concept replaces an exposed main rotor with a ducted ring, reducing blade signature while keeping a familiar rotary-wing silhouette. The ring itself doubles as a mounting surface for the aircraft's sensor band.

Every surface on the model was built to read clearly at low altitude and at a glance — a design study, not an engineering spec.

File 02 / 04Airframe

Angular low-profile hull

The fuselage trades curvature for faceted plating, breaking up the silhouette the way faceted stealth hulls do. The cockpit sits low and forward, tucked beneath the rotor shroud.
